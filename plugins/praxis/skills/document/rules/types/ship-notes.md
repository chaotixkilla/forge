# Ship notes

What a shipping run did to a change: where it landed and how it rolled out, for the reader who later asks "when did this reach production, and was it healthy?"

## Membership

The results of a shipping act: what merged into which line and the commit it merged as, the gate status, the environment and the rollout strategy with the exposure reached, and the health verdict with the signals behind it — or, where it didn't land or roll out, why.
