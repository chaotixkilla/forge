# Explanation

How a part of the system works and why it works that way, with a diagram where structure or flow carries the point better than prose.

## Membership

A result that says how the touched part behaves and why: a map of its flow, its states, the reasons behind its design. A page that starts telling the reader what to do is a [how-to](how-to.md); one that starts listing formats or settings is a [reference](reference.md).

System documentation says how one part of the system works now. It covers only what the task touched, is dated by the task, and is never edited after the task ends: a later task writes its own pages and links back. A page covers one part and one content type — no whole-system overviews — and every claim cites its source: code, documentation, a test result, a conversation. (basis: maintainer, 2026-09-30, after Diátaxis)
