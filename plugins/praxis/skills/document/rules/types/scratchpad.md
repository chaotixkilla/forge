# Scratchpad

The task's working notes: results its work produced on the way to a conclusion, such as a map of the area a change touches, evidence about the libraries it uses, the outcome of testing its assumptions, or a review's first pass. They're kept so a reader can check what a conclusion rested on.

## Membership

A result is a working note when a later result in the same task builds on it and it isn't itself delivered to anyone. A conclusion — a verdict, a decision, a finding delivered to someone — belongs to its own type. (basis: maintainer, 2026-09-30)

One scratchpad page per task, with a section per result in the order the results arrived, each dated and carrying its sources. A section keeps its result's own structure and grade names, minus any name of the machinery that produced it. (routed to maintainer: one page, since the notes are read together when checking a conclusion.)
