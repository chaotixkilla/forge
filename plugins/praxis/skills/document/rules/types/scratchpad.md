# Scratchpad

The task's working record: an account of how the claims its conclusions rest on were challenged — what the area a change touches does, what the libraries it uses actually do, whether its assumptions hold, what a review found before the author's explanation was read — kept so a reader can check what a conclusion rested on and how hard it was pushed.

Plumbing.

## Page

`own page`, one per round ([pages-belong-to-their-task](../pages-belong-to-their-task.md)), since a round's notes are read together, in the task's `Work history` group ([publish-and-link](../../phases/03-publish-and-link.md)). (routed to maintainer: one page per round; the group, maintainer, 2026-11-11)

## Membership

A result is a working note when a later result in the same task builds on it and it isn't itself delivered to anyone; a result not delivered and not yet built on is presumed one. A conclusion — a verdict, a decision, a finding delivered to someone — belongs to its own type. (basis: maintainer, 2026-09-30) Three sections are the exception. Two belong to a round of an act that authors a change, and are kept here under their fixed headings so the round's record can link them: `Review comments and their dispositions`, from the round's proposal, and `Record check`, the record check's verdict, with `Record check before the round closed` for the one made at its close. (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-07) The third is every round's `Brief`, as [run-the-act](../../../work/phases/03-run-the-act.md) keeps it, laid out as composed, not as claims. (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-19)

## Sections

One section per result. Its heading names what the result examined, with its date — "how the export job batches invoices" — never the work that produced it: the scratchpad's own heading rule, in place of a span ([type-files](../type-files.md)). The conclusions this page backs live on their own pages, so it opens straight on its sections. Each section leads with its claims that fail, then those that stay unsettled, then those that weren't checked, the rest following in the result's order. A claim fails when it failed its check: a defect finding the check upheld is a claim that holds, and one it refuted fails. A claim the result grades only for how sure it is — an entry in a map of the area, say — shows that grade and no word for whether it holds, and follows in the result's order. An item the result lists that is neither a graded claim nor left open — a map's divergences or external contracts, say — follows the claims, before the left-open items, in the result's order. An item the result lists as left open — a map's "what stays unknown", say — carries no word for whether it holds either, and the section's claims end on those items, in the result's order. (basis: derived — the result names them as not decided, so any word would regrade it) Each claim is a table row or list entry with:

- **the claim** — a map entry, an assumption, a finding, a library's behavior;
- **how it was tested or established** — the run, the read, the experiment, the source consulted;
- **what it showed**, in the result's own words, never regraded: whether it holds, fails, stays unsettled or wasn't checked (and why), and how sure, as certainty or support ([results-and-certainty](../../../../craft/evidence/results-and-certainty.md)); and, for a finding, its severity;
- **the evidence** — what was seen, with its source.

(basis: maintainer, 2026-10-02)

A claim whose content would make an item restricted ([items-and-agreements](../items-and-agreements.md)) goes, under its section's heading, to the task's restricted destination, and is held back while none is named, as a restricted item is ([shape-the-document](../../phases/02-shape-the-document.md)); the team page's line for it names the task's owner. (basis: benchmark, 2026-11-13, a scratchpad published in plain words what the threat model held back)

The account lays out what the result carries and reconstructs nothing: where the result doesn't say how a claim was tested or what it showed, that part reads "not recorded". What a result carries that isn't a claim — a follow-up, a next step — follows its claims as the result gives it. Timeline notes kept during a live incident stay in time order within their section, each entry with its evidence.
