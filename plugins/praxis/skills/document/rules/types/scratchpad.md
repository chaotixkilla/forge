# Scratchpad

The task's working record: an account of how the claims its conclusions rest on were challenged — what the area a change touches does, what the libraries it uses actually do, whether its assumptions hold, what a review found before the author's explanation was read — kept so a reader can check what a conclusion rested on and how hard it was pushed.

## Membership

A result is a working note when a later result in the same task builds on it and it isn't itself delivered to anyone. A conclusion — a verdict, a decision, a finding delivered to someone — belongs to its own type. (basis: maintainer, 2026-09-30)

## Shape

One scratchpad page per round ([pages-belong-to-their-task](../pages-belong-to-their-task.md)), with a section per result. (routed to maintainer: one page, since the notes are read together when checking a conclusion.)

A section is dated and headed by what it examined — "how the export job batches invoices" — never by the work that produced it. The conclusions this page backs live on their own pages, so it opens straight on its sections. Each section leads with its claims that fail, then those that stay unsettled, then those that weren't checked, the rest following in the result's order. A claim fails when it failed its check: a defect finding the check upheld is a claim that holds, and one it refuted fails. A claim the result grades only for how sure it is — an entry in a map of the area, say — shows that grade and no word for whether it holds, and follows in the result's order. An item the result lists that is neither a graded claim nor left open — a map's divergences or external contracts, say — follows the claims, before the left-open items, in the result's order. An item the result lists as left open — a map's "what stays unknown", say — carries no word for whether it holds either, and the section's claims end on those items, in the result's order. (basis: derived — the result names them as not decided, so any word would regrade it) Each claim is a table row or list entry with:

- **the claim** — a map entry, an assumption, a finding, a library's behavior;
- **how it was tested or established** — the run, the read, the experiment, the source consulted;
- **what it showed**, in the result's own words, never regraded: whether it holds, fails, stays unsettled or wasn't checked (and why), and how sure, as certainty or support ([results-and-certainty](../../../../craft/evidence/results-and-certainty.md)), or "not recorded" where the result carries no certainty word; and, for a finding, its severity;
- **the evidence** — what was seen, with its source.

(basis: maintainer, 2026-10-02)

The account lays out what the result carries and reconstructs nothing: where the result doesn't say how a claim was tested or what it showed, that part reads "not recorded". What a result carries that isn't a claim — a follow-up, a next step — follows its claims as the result gives it. Timeline notes kept during a live incident stay in time order within their section, each entry with its evidence.
