# Task record

Every other page of a task hangs beneath its task record, so a reader who has only the record can find the rest.

## Sections

In this order, each present even when empty; an empty section says so in a line.

1. **The task** — its key and title, the intent it serves (the ticket or requirement, linked, with its requirements and acceptance criteria as they stood when the task opened), and what kind of work it is: a review of a teammate's change, and which change.
2. **Status** — open or closed, what it's waiting on, and the date it was last updated.
3. **What was done** — each piece of work in the order it happened, in plain terms, each with where its result lives. Work that wasn't done is listed too, with why: the reason whoever decided gave, or the condition that ruled it out.
4. **Documents** — a link to every other page of the task, by type.
5. **What's next** — the next step, or "nothing: closed".

When the status changes, its section is rewritten, not appended to. The record links to the other pages rather than summarizing them ([one-type-per-page](../one-type-per-page.md)). (basis: maintainer, 2026-09-30)
