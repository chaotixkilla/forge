# Review record

The reviewer's own copy of a delivered review, kept whole; the change carries the comments.

## Membership

A review's final result: its brief, its findings and its questions for the author, each with where it was posted, or "not posted" and why. When the review ran in two passes, that's the second pass's result, with its marks of what reading the author's rationale changed. The working results the review built on — a map of the area, gathered evidence, test runs, the first pass — belong to the scratchpad, which the record links rather than copies. (basis: maintainer, 2026-09-30)
