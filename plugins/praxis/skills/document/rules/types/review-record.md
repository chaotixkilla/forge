# Review record

The reviewer's own copy of a delivered review, kept whole; the change carries the comments.

## Membership

A review's final result: its brief, its findings and its questions for the author, each with where it was posted, or "not posted" and why. When the review was formed before the author's explanation was read and then revisited with it, that's the revisited result, with the mark the review gives each item the explanation touched ([prior-pass](../../../review/modules/prior-pass.md)). The working results the review built on — a map of the area, gathered evidence, test runs, the findings formed before the author's explanation was read — belong to the scratchpad, which the record links rather than copies. (basis: maintainer, 2026-09-30)

Each round's review is a record of its own, about the change and commit the round reviewed ([pages-belong-to-their-task](../pages-belong-to-their-task.md)). (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-02)
