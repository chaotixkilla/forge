# Audit report

A security audit's account of a system's posture, for the system's owners.

## Membership

The security-review step's result in an auditing task: the surface audited and what was left out of it, the threat model, the findings ranked with their severity, confidence and reachability, and the posture they add up to. The message the act sends the owners links the report rather than restating it. (basis: derived from the deliverable of the act that files it)
