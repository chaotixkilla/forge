# Pages belong to their task

A task's documentation records what that task did and found, as of then. Once it's edited later it stops being a record of anything, because a reader can't tell what was known when.

## The rule

- Every page carries its key, round and last-updated date, in the footer [one-type-per-page](one-type-per-page.md) sets.
- A round has ended once it closes ([close-out](../../work/phases/04-close-out.md)). After that, none of its pages changes but for the place [publish-and-link](../phases/03-publish-and-link.md) gives it as later rounds join, its title when that phase renames it, and the titles its in-tree references name when a page they name is renamed; its section of the task history and its entry in the task record's Iterations are carried as they stand but for those references' titles. (basis: derived — a reference that kept an old title would point at nothing)
- The task record's current state, and the open round's Iterations entry, are the exception to all of this: it describes the task now, so every filing restates it. A round closes as [open-the-task](../../work/phases/02-open-the-task.md)'s Rounds says, and a decision record's status moving to accepted when the change ships is the one change a closed round's page takes besides those above. (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-07)
- A task's next round files its own pages beside the earlier rounds', so no round overwrites another, but for the task history, which adds the round's section to the one page: a type an earlier round filed is filed again as the new round's page, never by updating the earlier one.
- A later task that touches the same area writes its own pages and links back to the earlier ones. It never edits them.

(basis: maintainer, 2026-09-30; rounds, maintainer, 2026-10-02)
