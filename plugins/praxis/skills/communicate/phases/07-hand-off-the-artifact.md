The artifact is finished and verified ([tighten-and-verify](06-tighten-and-verify.md)). communicate doesn't send it anywhere: it hands it back, with everything the act delivering it needs to send it well.

## Return the artifact, with where it's meant to go

Return the finished artifact in the form [choose-form-and-channel](03-choose-form-and-channel.md) chose, together with:

- **its destination** — the channel or home that phase picked, and why;
- **its audience** — the tier it was pitched to, so a later reader knows who it was written for;
- **for a durable document**, a short fit-for-channel summary with a place for the link, ready for an announcement once the document is placed;
- **any follow-up the artifact owes** — a reply to watch for, a decision to record when it lands.

Returning is the whole of this phase's outcome, not a failure to deliver: many artifacts, such as a handoff message the user will place themselves, are correctly handed back. (basis: maintainer, 2026-09-30)

The return is a terminal report like any other skill's: lead with the artifact and where it's meant to go, and keep form, channel and tier machinery out of it ([deliver-at-the-readers-register](../../../craft/writing/deliver-at-the-readers-register.md)).
