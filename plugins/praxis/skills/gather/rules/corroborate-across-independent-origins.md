Corroboration raises confidence, but only when it's real — independent origins converging, not one origin echoed. Mistaking echoes for corroboration is how a weighted picture inflates a single voice into a consensus. (basis: the community-practices explorer's graded scale + anvil's community-practices "count origins not posts" craft, ratified 2026-07-03.)

## Count origins, not posts
1. Count distinct origins — authors not traceable to a common original — never raw post count. Ten posts citing one article are one origin echoed ten times; trace the echoes to their common source before counting.
2. Date every finding against the subject's change cadence, not the calendar: a finding predating the subject's last major or breaking version is presumed stale until re-confirmed.

## The anecdotal grade, and the independence tie-breaker

3. Independence grades *down* when unsure: two origins corroborate only when neither draws on the other. When you cannot tell a first-hand report from an echo of one already counted, treat it as one origin echoed and drop a rung — an unverified second origin is not corroboration.
4. Corroboration never crosses tiers: any number of independent anecdotal origins remains anecdotal, below a single authoritative source on the same claim. Corroboration strengthens signal *within* a tier; it never promotes across one.
