Gathering and verification produce a pile of graded claims; synthesis turns them into an answer. This is where the sub-questions get resolved into a coherent whole — conflicts reconciled where the evidence allows and represented where it doesn't, each conclusion weighted by the strength behind it and carrying the confidence it earned. A synthesis that averages disagreement into a confident middle, or lets an inference inherit its source's authority, is where a careful gather becomes an overstated answer.

## Weigh, don't tally

Assemble each sub-answer from its claims, weighting each claim by its source strength ([weight-by-source-strength](../rules/weight-by-source-strength.md)) — a single primary-authoritative source outweighs a chorus of community-anecdotal ones, and corroboration raises a claim's confidence within its tier but never promotes a weak source across one. Keep what a source *stated* distinct from what you *concluded* ([separate-claim-from-inference](../rules/separate-claim-from-inference.md)): an inference carries only the confidence your reasoning earns and stays at the strength of its weakest input, never the source's tier.

## Reconcile what resolves; represent what doesn't

Where sources conflict, resolve the conflict only when the evidence resolves it — one side clearly stronger, or newer where the field has genuinely moved ([watch-recency-and-drift](../rules/watch-recency-and-drift.md)) — and say why. Where neither side dominates, both stand, each with its backing, and the claim is graded **contested** ([surface-disagreement](../rules/surface-disagreement.md)): locate exactly where the dispute lives, and never manufacture a middle position neither source holds.

## Grade the answer and name the gaps

Give each sub-answer, and the top-line answer built from them, its grade on the [claim-confidence-scale](../rules/claim-confidence-scale.md) (established / corroborated / contested / unverified), from the corroboration and strength behind it. Then state, as plainly as the findings, what could not be established ([name-the-uncertainty](../rules/name-the-uncertainty.md)): the sub-questions that stayed open, the claims that stopped at a thin-evidence dead end ([know-when-to-stop](../rules/know-when-to-stop.md)), and the difference between "found no evidence" and "found evidence of no." An answer's overall confidence is bounded by its load-bearing claims — one unverified load-bearing claim caps the whole answer, it does not average out.

The output is the resolved answer: the top-line conclusion and its sub-answers, each graded and weighted, disagreements represented, gaps named — the substance [compose-output](06-compose-output.md) renders to the requested form.
