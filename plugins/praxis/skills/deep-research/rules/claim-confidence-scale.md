# The claim/answer-confidence scale

Every claim the report carries — and the answer built from them — states how sure it is. Confidence is what lets a reader act: rely on this, treat that as provisional, don't bet on the other. If the scale is undefined, each run grades on its own feel and the same evidence yields "established" in one report and "likely" in another. Confidence is **not** source strength ([weight-by-source-strength](weight-by-source-strength.md)): source strength is how strong *one source* is; confidence is how sure *the claim is true*, and it combines source strength with how many *independent* origins corroborate it and how *directly* they support the specific claim. Keep them separate — a single primary-authoritative spec can establish a claim alone, while ten echoing blogs establish nothing. This rule is cited from [verify-claims](../phases/04-verify-claims.md), [synthesize](../phases/05-synthesize.md), and [compose-output](../phases/06-compose-output.md).

## The four rungs

`(basis: the common core of how evidence frameworks grade confidence in a body of evidence — GRADE's high/moderate/low/very-low certainty and ICD 203's high/moderate/low analytic confidence, both driven by source quality × corroboration/consistency × directness, with ICD 203's explicit rule that confidence must not be conflated with source strength or with likelihood. The four-rung labels are the maintainer's proposed vocabulary.)`

- **established** — independent sources of adequate strength converge, and no credible contradiction stands; the claim is as settled as the evidence allows. Also reachable from a *single* source when that source is the definitive origin of the claim (a standard defining its own behavior establishes it alone).
  - *Anchor (top):* two independent vetted-secondary sources and the primary they both trace to agree on the value, and a deliberate counter-search ([guard-against-confirmation](guard-against-confirmation.md)) found no credible dissent.
- **corroborated** — at least two independent origins agree, but with a gap that keeps it short of established: the sources are mid-strength, a minor tension is unresolved, or the support is slightly indirect. Solid, not settled.
- **contested** — credible sources genuinely disagree, or credible support is met by a credible contradiction the evidence cannot resolve. The claim is delivered *as* a dispute, both sides represented ([surface-disagreement](surface-disagreement.md)), never collapsed to one.
- **unverified** — rests on a single origin, or on only community-anecdotal (tier-4) or weaker sources however many agree, or an unresolved or circular chain, or was not tested (`--verify=off`, or a budget/timebox cap cut verification short). Not necessarily false — just not established; delivered flagged, never upgraded to smooth the narrative ([name-the-uncertainty](name-the-uncertainty.md)).
  - *Anchor (bottom):* one community-anecdotal post asserts it, no independent origin found, the primary chain dead-ends.

## The adjacent-rung discriminators

- **established vs corroborated** — is there *no credible contradiction* AND (≥2 independent adequate-strength origins converging **or** a definitive primary origin)? Established. A gap in strength, directness, or an unresolved minor tension? Corroborated. (the promotion threshold, below.)
- **corroborated vs contested** — do the independent origins *agree* (corroborated) or does credible evidence *conflict* (contested)? The tell is a standing credible contradiction, not the absence of every doubt.
- **contested vs unverified** — is there credible evidence *on more than one side* (contested), or too little evidence to have sides at all — one origin, a broken chain, or untested (unverified)?

Count only **independent** origins ([triangulate-before-trusting](triangulate-before-trusting.md)): corroboration confirms that several parties *assert* a claim, not that it is true, so echoes of one origin do not raise a rung, and corroboration must attach to the *specific* claim, not an adjacent one. When two rungs seem to fit, the lower wins unless you can name the independent corroboration or the definitive origin that earns the higher.

## Grading a documented absence

Some answers are an *absence* — "no source establishes X," a gap in the evidence — and the rungs, built for a positive claim, still apply once you grade the right object: the claim about the **state of the evidence**, not the missing fact. A confirmed absence is **established** when an accountable source attests the gap (a review that states "no study exists") *and* a counter-search finds nothing that fills it ([guard-against-confirmation](guard-against-confirmation.md)); it stays **unverified** when you merely failed to find evidence and did not confirm the gap is real. It reaches established by the **definitive-origin path**, not the two-origins path: the accountable review is the *definitive origin* of the evidence-state claim — the authority on what the literature contains — and the counter-search supplies established's "no credible contradiction stands" condition rather than a second independent origin (your own null result is not an independent party, so it does not raise the rung by corroboration count — it confirms no contradiction stands). The discriminator is [name-the-uncertainty](name-the-uncertainty.md)'s: "the accountable review says no study exists, and I searched and found none" is a positive, corroborable finding; a bare "I found nothing" is unverified — never let the second read as the first.

## The promotion threshold

`(basis: ratified by the maintainer, 2026-07-13. A claim reaches **established** when either (a) ≥2 origins that are independent of each other and each at vetted-secondary strength or stronger (tiers 1–2 of [weight-by-source-strength](weight-by-source-strength.md)) converge on it with no credible contradiction standing, or (b) a single primary-authoritative source is the definitive origin of the claim; it is **corroborated** when ≥2 independent origins agree AND at least one reaches expert-attributed (tier 3) or stronger, but the established bar is unmet (the strongest agreeing source is only tier 3, or a minor gap is unresolved); and it stays **unverified** when the only agreement is among community-anecdotal (tier-4) or weaker sources — weak-tier corroboration does not lift a claim off unverified, consistent with [triangulate-before-trusting](triangulate-before-trusting.md) (any number of independent weak origins stays weak-tier). GRADE and ICD 203 fix the *shape* (strength × corroboration × directness) but not a general-purpose numeric cut, so the independent-origin count and the two strength floors (tier-3 for corroborated, tier-2 for established) are the maintainer's ratified house standard.)`
