The act file says which steps run, in what order, with which inputs; this phase carries that out without adding or dropping anything.

## Aim the work

Before a round's proposal, build its brief: the request, with what this task in particular calls for. (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-19)

- **When.** Once per round. A build, a follow-ups round, a ship round and a review round build one. A rework after peer review builds only what its review changes about the previous round's brief, composed from its comments without recruiting the curator, and added under it; one whose previous round kept no brief builds a whole one, as a build does. A revision keeps its round's brief, with its request and evidence added, written to the brief file and filed with its first step's filing. A rerun from the first step builds it again. A resume that goes to close-out reads the brief when there is one, and otherwise builds none. A resume that runs a step reads its round's brief from `.claude/praxis/briefs/<task>.md` in the main checkout when the file names this round, else from the round's filed `Brief` section, and with neither, builds one. When its marker records `answered`, as for a round opened before briefs existed, it builds without asking anything new, takes as settled each choice a step that ran has settled, leaves the rest assumptions, and is written and filed with the next step's filing; otherwise it builds as a new round does. An act file whose Before the proposal section says the brief waits, or withholds an input below, is followed.
- **Gather.** Recruit the [curator](../../../agents/explorers/curator.md), handing it:
  - the request in the user's words, the intent [open-the-task](02-open-the-task.md) read, and the act;
  - the memory folder;
  - the task's own key, the keys its entry links as related, and the keys of the entries whose `parent` is the task's ([keep-the-task-memory-entry](../rules/keep-the-task-memory-entry.md)), or, when the fetch couldn't expose a parent, that tasks under the same parent item weren't searched; earlier tasks in the same area it finds itself, by searching their records;
  - every other open task's key, with its branch;
  - the act file's Before the proposal section, where it has one.

  Without fan-out, this context follows the curator's method itself, keeping for the brief only what that method would return. A source it couldn't reach is named in the brief, and never makes the proposal ask.
- **Compose.** The request heads the brief, word for word, never paraphrased, since every step would inherit a paraphrase's drift. Under it, a line goes in only when leaving it out would cause a mistake on this task: it changes what a step does, how deeply, or what the record must show. A lens that finds nothing leaves no line. Lines usually come from these, and from anything else that passes the test:
  - the outcome the intent wants, and its motivation;
  - where the change lands, and the existing code doing the same kind of thing, labelled **existing**, apart from what is **to build**;
  - what makes this round done, as checks a step can run;
  - what the request leaves out of scope;
  - the change's kinds, by the [documentation-kit](../../../craft/writing/documentation-kit.md)'s rows, and its risk tier by the [change-risk-scale](../../../craft/engineering/change-risk-scale.md), graded on the intended change and marked provisional;
  - what the record must show: each kind's evidence, with the moment it must be captured, a capture of the base, such as a before screenshot or the base data model, coming before the first change;
  - which steps run deeper, by the tier (below);
  - obligations and traps, each with its source, and files another open task changes too. A delivery preference the curator returns that departs from the act file's Delivered section governs that part of the delivery, the act's own being the default for a user who recorded none; its line names the part it replaces, or the delivery it adds, so close-out follows it without asking. One that would leave the act's Done when unreachable, as never commenting on a change under review, is a conflicting obligation for the proposal instead (below), its default holding that part of the delivery, the cautious side, so the round waits on its owner rather than posting against their preference (basis: derived from [ask-while-the-user-is-here](../../gather/rules/ask-while-the-user-is-here.md)'s cautious default) (basis: field findings, 2026-10-28 — five runs met a delivery preference only at delivery);
  - each claim an earlier task made about the code, as that task's claim, marked to check: the round's first step to run takes the brief without these lines, so it forms its own view before reading them ([form-your-own-view-first](../../../craft/evidence/form-your-own-view-first.md));
  - the choices the intent leaves open, each with the step that settles it. A choice the owner answered, or that took its default, binds that step unless the owner reverses it, and the step's result states the reading, its alternatives and why it was picked, "taken by default" with its reason for a default (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-20). When the step can't build such a choice so it meets the round's done checks and every obligation in the brief, a better fit alone not counting, it records why and stops blocked, the owner's call the task's next step. An assumption the proposal didn't ask about is the step's to settle, and its result replaces the assumption in the brief, written and filed with that step's filing;
  - what the act file's Before the proposal section adds.
- **Depth by risk.** The tier picks among settings the act's steps already have, never an instruction to check again, since a strong model told to re-check over-verifies with no gain. `contained` keeps the act's settings. `bounded` runs review at high rigor for each unit whose change touches a surface, as the change-risk scale reads "touched", in the reviewing act its steps 4 and 5, and gives verify, beside its own inputs, every flow a member of the change's consumer set sits on. `exposed` does that for every unit, and, when the change is of the documentation kit's security-relevant kind, has maintaining run its security review as though the request asked for one; review already runs its security lens in every act. It also has the plan state a rollback criterion, or, in an act with no plan step, lists a rollback criterion among what the record must show. The tier never adds a heavyweight flag on its own. When a step's result shows the reach other than the brief graded it, wider or narrower, regrade by the scale. The new grade is written into the brief file and filed with that step's filing, and the steps after it take its settings without asking again. (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-20 — no tool or study scales an agent's verification by risk, so the settings are the maintainer's)
- **Keep.** Once the proposal is settled, with each answered choice settled in it, write the brief, headed by its round, to `.claude/praxis/briefs/<task>.md` in the main checkout under the task's final key, replacing an earlier round's with this round's whole brief, and file it with the opening filing, or the fold's filing that stands in for it, as the round's scratchpad section headed `Brief`. Under it go the proposal's answers that later phases read: each item that took its default ([ask-while-the-user-is-here](../../gather/rules/ask-while-the-user-is-here.md)), but a comment's disposition, which its own section keeps. A declined proposal writes none. A reply that steers one of its assumptions before the step that settles it has run updates the brief, written and filed with the next step's filing. After that step, it is an input asking for a change ([edits-change-intent-not-actions](../rules/edits-change-intent-not-actions.md)). Under `--dry-run`, the curator runs and the brief is shown, and nothing is written.

## Propose, then run

Before proposing, check that each step's skill is available and that the ports the act files and delivers through, a delivery preference's own included, are configured, with the artifacts home's destination set where the backend uses one. Show the brief beside them in short: its kinds and risk, what the record must show, the steps that run deeper, its obligations and traps, and its open choices as the assumptions the run will take, each of which the user can steer by replying. (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-07) Exactly these make it ask, and the question holds each that applies: an act [route-the-work](01-route-the-work.md) took on an unclear fit, or a request with two readings that would give different acceptance criteria, offered as the reading the run would take; up to three of the brief's open choices whose readings would give a different data model, a different interface or a different set of units, ranked by the higher of the change-risk scale's tiers their readings would build, the higher first, a tie going to the choice with fewer anchored brief lines behind its default, the rest, and every choice inside one unit whose readings differ in no interface or data model outside it, staying assumptions; every open choice's default, asked or not, being the reading that follows a pattern the existing code the brief names holds consistently, two or more places doing it one way and none another, since consistency is a reason of its own; else the reading of the lower change-risk tier, else the smaller change ([smallest-reversible-change](../../../craft/engineering/smallest-reversible-change.md)) (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-20) (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-19 — at most three, after a specification tool's cap on the questions a spec carries); between two of one class, a preference and a recorded decision counting as one, the nearer instruction file's, else the later one's (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-20 — current words over a standing preference, and the repository's rule over both); and a rework after peer review's comment dispositions (below). The question puts each item as [ask-while-the-user-is-here](../../gather/rules/ask-while-the-user-is-here.md) says, a bare go-ahead naming no choice. The defaults: the reading the run would take, running without the port, running without the heavyweight flag, a stack or fold as open-the-task says, the request's own words as the intent, and an unavailable skill's step recorded `skipped-by-user` with the reason that its skill isn't available. An answer naming another act declines this proposal and routes the request again with that act ([route-the-work](01-route-the-work.md)), as a new task with the next free number, the declined task passed over. (basis: derived — a blanket go-ahead isn't approval of a cost, so the cheaper side is the default) The answer settles each heavyweight step's flag, so its skill doesn't ask again. A setup the answer chose runs right after it, interactively — the one exception to asking nothing after that question, since the user chose it in answering — before any deferred part of "Before the steps" and the first step. Running without a port is this task's choice: an act never records a capability as unused for the project. The user may remove steps; record each removal with the user's reason, in their own words if they give no other. Adding, reordering or re-inputting steps isn't offered. (basis: maintainer, 2026-09-30) (routed to maintainer: removal only, since a changed sequence is a different act.)

A rework after peer review folds into its proposal each comment its round takes as input, with a proposed disposition: addressed, by the change it names; declined, with a reason; or deferred, to where it goes. A rework always asks, since its owner confirms the dispositions, though a revision of a rework round is shown as a build's is ([open-the-task](02-open-the-task.md)). (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-07) The question says that a proposed decline or deferral stands only when the answer names that comment; every other comment is addressed, so the run never declines or defers one on its own. A comment addressed that changes a requirement, conflicting with the spec or adding to it, is taken up as the act file's rework says; an act with no spec step records the changed requirement with the comment's disposition in its scratchpad section. (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-07) The settled dispositions, and every other choice the owner made in answering with its reason, go with the opening filing as the round's scratchpad section headed `Review comments and their dispositions`, each disposition with its comment's id, one taken by default marked as [ask-while-the-user-is-here](../../gather/rules/ask-while-the-user-is-here.md) says. (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-07; the proposed default, derived from the spec and decisions the task already settled) When the proposal asks, only the user's reply to the question as shown answers it: a go-ahead in the request, or one given before the steps were shown, doesn't. (basis: derived from the act being proposed and asked once, maintainer, 2026-09-30) The user may stop the act or drop steps by replying at any point: a dropped step not yet run is recorded `skipped-by-user` with their reason. ## Keep the record

A step ends when its skill returns and every explorer and critic it recruited has returned: a step whose agents are still out is still running, its `waiting` set to `agents` ([keep-the-act-marker](../rules/keep-the-act-marker.md)). An agent that ends in an error or with no result has returned with none: the step's result names the gap, as a lane not reached. An agent's return that comes back after its skill returned joins the step's result unchanged, as that agent's return; the step's work is not redone. A result from an agent the step counted as returned with none that still arrives after the step was recorded is a finding on that step's result, filed into it (below); when close-out's question was already shown, it is shown again with what changed. (basis: field findings, 2026-10-28 — late critics overturned filed results and a shown stance) Its result showing in the conversation, as a skill's closing report does, doesn't end the step's work, or the act: what follows here comes before anything else is said or done, and before the turn ends. A result that reached only the conversation is lost to the record. (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-07) Where the act file says how results are recorded while documentation must not slow the work, as the incident act does while an incident is live, that governs instead. (basis: maintainer, 2026-09-30) After each step, record how it ended, on the act's checklist and in the act marker, using exactly the outcomes in [skip-only-with-a-reason](../rules/skip-only-with-a-reason.md): the marker's `outcome` becomes `ran`, `skipped-by-act` or `skipped-by-user`, and a skip's `reason` holds the act's condition or the user's reason. The act file says whether the run continues past a step that broke down. When the act stops early, every step it didn't reach is `skipped-by-act`, with the stop as its reason, and close-out still runs. A step that runs again, as a loop back to an earlier step makes it, has its `filed` set back to `false` before it runs. (basis: derived from a rerun's result being a new result) Skips recorded together, as an early stop's unreached steps, go to document in one filing, each with its step key, outcome and reason. When it fails, each step's piece is written to that step's own file. (basis: derived — one filing for skips that carry no result of their own)

Then hand [document](../../document/SKILL.md) the task key, the task's memory entry, the step's key and result (for a skipped step, its outcome and reason), with what it ran for when the act runs it more than once: its unit, or the review requests it covered, every document filed for the task so far, and every file waiting in the task's unfiled directory ([keep-the-act-marker](../rules/keep-the-act-marker.md)). After every step, bring the entry's status, next step and commit up to date — the commit being the head the step left the task's work at — and its index line's status ([keep-the-task-memory-entry](../rules/keep-the-task-memory-entry.md)). Record the filing in the act marker as keep-the-act-marker says: what document hands back goes to the unfiled directory only when the filing fails, and a filing is never deferred to a later one. (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-07) A filing handed to an agent that runs on while the act goes on, or queued behind another, sets the step's `waiting` as keep-the-act-marker says, and the next step doesn't wait for it; a filing agent that ends in an error or with no result is a failed filing; two filings of one task's record run one after the other, never at once. (basis: field findings, 2026-10-28) A result document declines as matching no type ([shape-the-document](../../document/phases/02-shape-the-document.md)) is no failed filing: the step's `filed` becomes `true`, and the run's report names the result. (basis: derived — nothing a later filing could publish waits) With the artifacts port one the user chose to run without, each filing is still handed to document. (basis: derived from an unconfigured port's filing failing like any other) Whenever document returns the task record's location, as a failed filing can too, write it into the entry's docs link, and set the marker's `docs` to it with its reach and share line. Keep each page's location document returns with that page, and hand it back with the page on the next filing; pages fetched when the round opened keep theirs. When the result wasn't filed, say so in the run's report, and why. Report each advisory document returns, whether the result was filed or not, an in-tree reference that didn't resolve among them.

**On to the next step.** Once a step is recorded and its filing handed on or queued, start the next step in the same turn ([ask-while-the-user-is-here](../../gather/rules/ask-while-the-user-is-here.md)). The act stops between steps only when the user says stop, a step stops blocked (Aim the work, above), the act file's rule for a step that broke down stops it, or one of the questions After the proposal names (above) is asked. A question waits for its answer and the act goes on from it. A blocked step, like the user's stop, leaves the act where it stands for a later run (Propose, then run); a step that broke down where the act file stops is the early stop above. The report says what would let the act go on. A turn that ends while a step's `waiting` is set picks up where that wait ends. (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-23 — acts stalled after every step until the user said "go")

Findings made outside the steps are filed too, by these rules. (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-07)

- **What a finding is.** A sourced claim the task's record doesn't already hold, such as new evidence, a correction or a new result, made while the task is open, or about what a closed task of an act that authors a change changed, typically in answer to the user. An answer that explains or restates what the record holds isn't one. (basis: derived from the record holding what the task found)
- **Where it goes.** A finding that changes a result a step of the current round filed or holds waiting, by correcting a claim in it, adding evidence to it or redoing what it did, goes into the latest version of the result of the earliest such step that ran, a waiting one included. That result is handed to document again, with its step key and what it ran for. A finding that changes an earlier round's result is a result of its own. Any other finding is handed to document as a result of its own, with no step key, so document types it by its membership test. A result labelled `attached:`, `research:`, `moved:`, `pending:`, `revision:` or `outside:` ([side-work-asks-once](../rules/side-work-asks-once.md), [help-build-the-mental-model](../rules/help-build-the-mental-model.md), [open-the-task](02-open-the-task.md)'s A revision and Reconcile what happened outside praxis) is always a result of its own, never merged into a step's, and carries its label to document. (basis: derived — a correction replaces the claim it corrects, instead of standing beside it)
- **When it's filed.** While this session runs the task's act, it is filed with the filing of the step it changes, or, when it has no step key, with the filing of the latest step that ran, that step's result handed again with its key and what it ran for. That step's `filed` goes back to `false` until the filing lands. A finding made before any step has run goes with the opening filing, or, where none runs, as on a fold or a resume, with the first step's filing. For a revision ([open-the-task](02-open-the-task.md)), a stop before its first change, its conflict update or its start step's skill, or the question's answer declining it, is its declined proposal: its marker is removed, nothing is filed, the entry's status goes back to the wait on review, and its round stays open. A later stop leaves a stopped revision. Any other declined proposal, the question's answer declining it or a stop before any step has started, ends the act unrun: every step is recorded `skipped-by-user`, replacing any outcome already recorded, its reason `proposal declined:` and the user's words, and filed as skips recorded together are, and [close-out](04-close-out.md) then closes the round, as its Close the task says; a finding made in that reply goes with that filing. When a filing carrying a finding with no step key fails, that finding's pieces wait in `finding.md` ([keep-the-act-marker](../rules/keep-the-act-marker.md)). (basis: derived — every finding lands in a filing the marker or `finding.md` keeps)
- **With this session running no act for the task,** as after close-out while the task waits on a review, it is filed through [open-the-task](02-open-the-task.md)'s route for a finding.

A step skipped because the request already carries its result, such as a cause already shown or an approved spec, hands document that result with its source, beside the skip's outcome and reason, when the result exists only in the conversation. One that lives in a document is linked instead. (basis: derived from a skip reason alone leaving out of the record the result it names) The task's documentation changes only through document. A correction the user asks for on a published page is a finding, filed by the rules above, never an edit made directly on the backend, which would skip the round, footer and log rules a filing applies. (basis: maintainer, 2026-10-07)

Under `--dry-run`, see [dry-run](../modules/dry-run.md).
