# typed-findings (`--typed`)

Activated by `--typed`, referenced from [deliver-findings](../phases/06-deliver-findings.md).

The base review returns its findings as prose a person reads. This module additionally emits them to the **harness's typed finding channel** — the structured review surface the host renders itself — so the findings arrive as records a tool can filter, rank, and carry across runs rather than as text something would have to parse. Deletion test: remove it and the review's prose report is unchanged; the delta is the structured emission beside it.

## The delta

- **Emit the ranked findings as typed records, in addition to the report.** The prose report is **always** produced as the record ([deliver-findings](../phases/06-deliver-findings.md)); `--typed` adds a channel, it never replaces one. Emit the same floored, triaged list the report shows — the flag changes where findings go, never which findings survive.
- **Map review's pinned record onto the typed record, field for field.** Each finding already carries a severity, a confidence, a `file:line`, what is wrong, the failing scenario or concrete cost, and the smallest suggested fix. Every one of those must arrive: the location and the severity as the channel's own fields where it offers them, and any field the channel has no slot for **folded into the record's prose body rather than dropped**. A field lost in translation is this module's characteristic defect — the typed record then looks complete while carrying less than the report it came from, and a consumer ranking on it ranks on less than the reviewer knew.
- **What is not a finding stays in the prose report.** The reader's brief and the scope line describe the review rather than a defect, so they have no per-finding home and are not synthesized into one. They remain in the report, which is why the report is always produced — the typed channel carries the findings, not the review's account of itself.
- **Scope findings are excluded from the emission and stay in the report.** A scope finding carries **no severity and no confidence** by design — it is a boundary note, not a graded defect ([deliver-findings](../phases/06-deliver-findings.md)). A typed record that wants a severity per finding would force one onto it, and inventing a grade the [severity-scale](../rules/severity-scale.md) never defined is worse than not emitting the note at all: it would put a bundled-but-unrelated change on the same ladder as a defect in the change under review. Emit the graded findings only, and say in the report that the scope notes were not emitted, so their absence from the channel is legible rather than silent.
- **Degrade like every other sink.** If the channel is unavailable or the emission fails, fall back to the prose report and say plainly that the findings were not emitted — the same posture `--comment` takes when its backend is unreachable. A failed emission never fails the review.
- **Composition is the general rule, not a special case.** `--typed` is one more independent sink over the one shared floored list, and it composes with `--comment`, `--fix`, and `--gate` exactly as [deliver-findings](../phases/06-deliver-findings.md) already pins: under `--fix`, emit what remains after fixing, so the channel and the gate read the same post-fix list; no sink redefines severity or the floor.

`(basis: derived — the mapping obligations follow from review's record already being pinned: a channel that carries fewer fields than the pinned record silently narrows the finding, so the fold-rather-than-drop rule is what keeps the two channels saying the same thing. The scope-finding exclusion is forced by this skill's own pin that such a note carries no grade; given a channel that requires one, excluding the note is the only option that neither fabricates a severity nor hides that something was withheld.)`
